SETTINGS

Listening

Always listening uses its own microphone and voice detection, so there is no beep. It sends only your speech, after you pause, to Groq for transcription (free limits apply). Keep the screen on and the app open. Say "Oliver" first, or turn Wake word OFF to answer everything you say.

Voice

Pick the voice that sounds most like OLIVER. The list comes from your phone, so you may see a few British options. Lower pitch and a slightly slower pace give a calmer, butler-like tone.

Voice check: tap Test voice.

Gemini voice needs a free Gemini key (aistudio.google.com) in the field below. Free limits are small and can change. If it runs out, OLIVER falls back to the phone voice automatically.

Live data

Weather and crypto prices work with no key. For stocks, S&P 500 and company prices, add a free key from finnhub.io.

Install

Add OLIVER to your home screen so it opens full screen like an app.

Calls, texts and Gmail

OLIVER can open the dialer, a text, or a Gmail draft ready to send. You tap once to finish it. A web app cannot dial or send by itself.
